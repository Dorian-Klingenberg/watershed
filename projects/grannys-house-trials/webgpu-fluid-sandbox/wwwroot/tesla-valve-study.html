The terrain selector contains one Tesla map: the user-approved blue-route stamp. It preserves the drawn route cell for cell without inventing extra branches.

Top-down map

Use the map

Open the app, choose Terrain → Tesla valve · stamped blue-route map, then run or step the simulation. The initial pour starts at the left port near (8, 64). Right-click a channel cell to place a spring; repeated clicks there increase its flow. Forward and reverse reservoir controls remain available for comparing routes.

Physical context

A Tesla valve is a fixed conduit, not a check valve. Its repeated return passages redirect reverse flow and increase losses, while the preferred direction follows a more direct path. This depth-averaged shallow-water playground can show route interactions, but it does not resolve three-dimensional boundary layers, separated jets, or turbulent mixing that drive much of the real device's directional resistance.
