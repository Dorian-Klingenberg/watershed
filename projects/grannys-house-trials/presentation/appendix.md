# Optional background

Keep this page out of the main presentation unless someone asks how the
experiments fit together.

## What the current work is

- **The game idea:** explore a world shaped by old water systems, understand a
  local problem, try a repair, and learn from its effects.
- **The 3D yard prototype:** a D3D12-based prototype includes a small,
  deterministic drainage round. Its goal is to water the north garden without
  making the cellar edge wet or the path soft.
- **The WebGPU water-flow workbench:** a standalone browser app for exploring
  water flow over editable terrain. It is not built on the yard prototype and
  is not a finished game scene.
- **The images:** concept art and map studies help us discuss possibilities.
  They are not screenshots of completed gameplay.

The presentation deliberately leaves the wider design open. The yard and
water-flow experiments are ways to learn what is understandable and enjoyable,
not a claim that every part of the eventual game has been decided.
